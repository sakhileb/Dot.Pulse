This Privacy Policy explains how **BluePin Inc** ("BluePin", "we", "us", "our"), the company responsible for Dot.Pulse, collects, uses, stores, and shares personal information when you use Dot.Pulse and the wider Dot Ecosystem it connects to. It is written to align with South Africa's **Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("POPIA")**.

## 1. Who we are

BluePin Inc is the responsible party for the personal information Dot.Pulse processes. Our Information Officer can be reached at privacy@infodot.co.za for any question, request, or concern about your personal information.

## 2. What we collect

**Account information** — your name, email address, and password (stored as a salted hash, never in plain text), via your Dot Ecosystem account.

**Profile and activity** — your public profile, the communities you join, and your reputation points, badges, and streaks.

**Content you post** — discussions, questions, articles, and every other content type you publish, along with comments, reactions, hashtags, and any media you upload. Most content on Dot.Pulse is public by design — it's a community platform, not a private workspace.

**Direct messages** — messages you send in a conversation are visible only to the participants of that conversation.

**Reports and moderation records** — if you report a post or comment, or if your content is reviewed by moderation, we keep a record of that action.

**Technical information** — IP address, browser and device information, and session activity, collected automatically for security and to keep you signed in.

## 3. AI content moderation

Every new post is automatically screened by an AI moderation service before it's fully visible in the feed. The post's content is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate a spam score, safety score, sentiment, summary, tags, and topics, which determines whether the post is approved, flagged for human review, or rejected. If the AI service is unavailable, a post is held for moderation rather than published unmoderated. This scoring is used only to keep the community safe and useful — it isn't used for advertising or shared outside this moderation and knowledge-extraction purpose.

## 4. Why we process your information

We process personal information to:

- create and maintain your account, and authenticate you when you sign in;
- let you sign in once and move between connected Dot Ecosystem platforms without re-entering your credentials;
- let you post, comment, react, follow other members, and message directly;
- moderate content to keep the community safe;
- compute reputation, badges, and streaks based on your accepted solutions and activity; and
- keep Dot.Pulse secure and prevent abuse.

## 5. Ecosystem single sign-on

When you use another Dot Ecosystem platform to sign in to Dot.Pulse (or vice versa), a short-lived, single-use authentication token confirms who you are without exposing your password to the connected platform.

## 6. Who can see your information

Your public profile, posts, comments, and reactions are visible to other Dot.Pulse members (and, for posts in public communities, potentially to the wider public). Direct messages are visible only to the people in that conversation. Reports you submit are visible to moderators only. We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share your content outside Dot.Pulse except where you've made it public by posting it.

## 7. How long we keep your information

We keep your account, profile, and content data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove your personal information, though some content (like a comment that's part of a public discussion) may be retained in de-identified form to preserve conversation context, and moderation/report records may be kept longer where needed for safety and legal reasons.

## 8. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including encrypted password storage, participant-scoped access to direct messages, fail-safe (not fail-open) content moderation, and single-use SSO tokens for ecosystem sign-in. No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Your rights under POPIA

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

- request access to the personal information we hold about you;
- request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information;
- request deletion of your personal information;
- object to our processing of your personal information in certain circumstances; and
- lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

To exercise any of these rights, contact privacy@infodot.co.za.

## 10. Cookies

Dot.Pulse uses a session cookie to keep you signed in. See our Cookie Policy for details.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you directly.
